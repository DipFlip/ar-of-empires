THE FIELD MANUAL

Paper into a kingdom.

  1. Print the kit at 100%. Lay the 5 × 7 field flat, with good, even lighting.
  2. Point your rear camera at the field. Keep several tags visible. The castle appears in the centre.
  3. Place Tag A–J on the same surface. Each becomes an archer tower. Move the paper to move the tower.
  4. Begin the siege. Towers aim and fire automatically within two tag spacings (80 mm). Survive six waves.

Keep towers flat on the field plane. A covered or removed tower becomes inactive after a short grace period. Combat pauses when the field leaves view.

In the demo, tap a letter to add or remove a tower, then drag it across the battlefield.
